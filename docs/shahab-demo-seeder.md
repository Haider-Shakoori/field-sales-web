# Shahab Kabul Demo Seeder

The Shahab demo dataset is intentionally opt-in because it is large.

## Run

```bash
php artisan migrate
php artisan db:seed --class=Database\\Seeders\\ShahabDemoSeeder
```

Alternatively set:

```dotenv
SEED_SHAHAB_DEMO=true
```

before running the normal `DatabaseSeeder`.

## Seeded scale

- 4 Shahab operational sales zones in Kabul, each with a geofence polygon
- 22 Kabul municipal district territories
- 4 sales managers
- 4 supervisors
- 52 salesmen
  - 11 non-route salesmen per zone
  - 2 routed salesmen per zone
- 8 sales routes
- 200 customers per district (4,400 total)
- Direct salesman assignment for every customer
- Near-equal customer distribution among the 13 salesmen in each zone
- 100 completed visits per salesman in the most recently completed week (5,200 visits)
- 20 Shahab FMCG products and a retail price list

## Demo geography and contact-data rules

Customer coordinates are deterministic synthetic points distributed around representative public-area centers for the relevant Kabul district. District and zone polygons are approximate demo geofences, not official cadastral boundaries.

Names are synthetic Afghan male demo identities. Phone values use an Afghanistan-style placeholder pattern generated by the seeder. They are not scraped contacts and should not be treated as verified real-person phone numbers.

Historical visit records reference inactive seeded devices so the one-active-device policy does not block a salesperson from signing in on a real demo phone.
